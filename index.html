Inky Quest

A bounce puzzle by Teko Labs

Steer Inky as she bounces, match her colour to break the tiles, then take the pearls.

Inky the octopus bounces up and down by herself. You only steer, left and right. A tile breaks when Inky is its colour, paint changes the colour she is, and once every tile is gone the clams open and give up their pearls.

From there it becomes a puzzle. Kelp lets Inky through only in its own colour. Keys open the locks of their colour. Currents flow one way, whirlpools carry Inky across the reef, and crabs can be pushed but never pulled. The later levels have to be worked out before she is launched.

Where to get it

Inky Quest is a game for phones, held sideways. It is being tested now and is coming to Android first, with iPhone to follow. It is free, with no adverts and nothing to buy.

Privacy

The game keeps your progress on your own device. It sends nothing about you anywhere unless you say it may send error reports. The whole of it is in the privacy policy.
